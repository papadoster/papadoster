## Research

**Robot learning under inference delay** · [jit-reflex](https://github.com/papadoster/jit-reflex)

Vision-language-action robot policies plan a chunk of actions from one camera frame and
then act blind until the next call returns. I study what cheap, training-free feedback can
recover between calls when the target moves, and how the answer depends on inference delay.
Experiments are pre-registered, and negative results are reported next to positive ones.
